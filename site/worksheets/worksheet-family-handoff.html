Triad setup

Doorway script — Receiver self-check

☐ 1. Named myself and my role

☐ 2. Asked for the pattern, not the plot ("compared with yesterday?")

☐ 3. Mined the everyday three (appetite, sleep, mood)

☐ 4. Wrote something down in view of the family

Observer checklist

☐ Asked at least one comparative question

☐ Anchored at least one untimed event ("before or after lunch?")

☐ Ran a teach-back ending with "What did I miss?"

☐ Never said a single word that sounded like medical advice

☐ Treated caregiver guesses as data, not verdicts
